# Ashbi messaging matrix

Status: Proposed. This controls descriptions, not unsupported proof claims.

## Company message

| Context | Approved working language |
| --- | --- |
| One line | Human-led brand, commerce, and business systems for growing consumer brands and selected service businesses. |
| Short | Ashbi connects identity, packaging, websites, custom software, and carefully governed automation so a business can look distinctive, sell clearly, and operate with less friction. |
| Expanded | Bianca's hands-on creative and production craft combines with Cameron's web, software, and systems practice. We build the brand and customer experience, then improve the workflows behind it where modern platforms, integrations, and human-supervised AI can solve a defined problem. |

Do not publish the expanded language until the company charter and capability states are approved.

## Audience messages

| Audience | Lead with | Supporting capability | Avoid leading with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CPG and DTC founder | One coherent product brand from identity and pack to storefront | Production artwork, launch creative, managed support | AI transformation |
| Brand or marketing lead | Senior hands-on creative, connected rollout, dependable production files | Commerce, content workflow, ongoing support | Framework names |
| Ecommerce team | Brand-consistent customer journey and maintainable product content | Shopify, headless options, integrations | Guaranteed conversion lifts |
| Service-business owner | A modern customer journey connected to the way the business operates | Portal, internal tool, automation pilot | CPG specialization as if it applies to them |
| Operations leader | A bounded workflow improvement with ownership, controls, and fallback | Custom platform, reporting, governed AI | Autonomous replacement claims |
| Upwork prospect | The exact implementation or systems problem Cameron can solve | Ashbi infrastructure and retained support where relevant | Bianca's creative portfolio unless it fits the brief |

## Service language

| Service | Promise | State language |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand and packaging | Build a distinctive, usable system from identity through production-ready packaging. | Current |
| Websites and commerce | Create a clear, fast, maintainable customer experience connected to content and commerce operations. | Current |
| Custom platforms | Replace a constrained workflow with a focused portal, tool, integration, or application that the client can operate. | Current for selected scoped work; evidence varies |
| AI and automation | Test a repetitive information workflow with human supervision, auditability, and a manual fallback. | Pilot |
| Managed support | Provide accountable creative, website, product, or systems continuity after launch. | Current; managed automation remains Pilot |

## Channel roles

- `ashbi.ca`: lead with CPG/DTC creative specialization, connected commerce, verified work, and qualification. Introduce systems as a restrained secondary path until pilots create proof.
- `hub.ashbi.ca`: use the full internal service taxonomy for qualification, scope, ownership, delivery, and reporting.
- Cameron Ashley and Upwork: lead with the exact web, software, platform, or systems problem; remain professionally compatible with Cameron's employment and never imply University endorsement.
- Proposals: describe only the approved scope, assumptions, exclusions, and evidence relevant to that prospect.
- Content: teach from operated experience and source material; label hypotheses and pilots honestly.

## Prohibited language without evidence

- Guaranteed revenue, conversion, SEO, time savings, or platform replacement.
- “AI transformation consultancy,” “autonomous agency,” or equivalent established-market claim.
- Invented years of experience, client counts, awards, testimonials, partner status, or performance metrics.
- Client names, marks, files, or results without display permission and contribution accuracy.
- “Full service” when the work has no named owner, delivery model, or boundary.

## CTA hierarchy

Primary: Discuss a brand, packaging, commerce, or connected launch project.  
Secondary: Explore a website, platform, or systems opportunity.  
Retention: Review ongoing creative, website, product, or systems support.

All booking-duration labels must resolve to one approved duration before release.
