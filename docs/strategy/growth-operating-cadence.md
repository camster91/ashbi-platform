# Ashbi growth operating cadence

Status: Proposed for Cameron and Bianca approval

## Purpose

Turn recorded demand into a small number of owned marketing and sales actions without inventing attribution, automating representational outreach, or creating another tracker. The Hub is the operating record; this document defines the review method.

## Weekly review

Use the staff **Inquiries** workspace and complete the review in this order:

1. Resolve every overdue or unscheduled active follow-up. These action counts always cover all active leads and are never narrowed by the acquisition window.
2. Open the **Last 30 days** window and review missing attribution. Correct it only from source evidence; never infer a source from a name, company, or message.
3. Compare **Demand by service**, **Lead sources**, and **Current stages** with the **Last 90 days** window to distinguish a recent signal from the broader recorded pattern.
4. Review each new inquiry for fit, assign one account owner, and record one dated next human action before moving it into an active stage.
5. Select one primary acquisition action for the coming week. Examples include one evidence-backed case-study draft, one service-page improvement, one approved Upwork proposal batch, or one bounded prospecting experiment.
6. Attest that source coverage was reviewed, missing attribution remains disclosed, and monetary evidence was kept currency-separated. Record whether the selected action requires no external approval, remains pending, was separately approved, or was declined.
7. Use **Weekly growth action** to create the resulting work as one owned Hub task under the internal Growth project. Do not create a parallel spreadsheet or free-standing task list.

The handoff permits one task per organization and Monday-starting week. An exact replay reuses the existing task; a different action for the same week is reported as a conflict instead of overwriting its evidence. The task records fresh 30-day and 90-day summaries, the action, owner, due date, and approval boundary. Code presence or a generated task does not prove that a weekly review occurred; the review counts only after the team actually completes the evidence steps above.

After four consecutive tasks are completed within their own review weeks, use the read-only `export:growth-cadence-evidence` command documented in [unified-launch-readiness.md](../unified-launch-readiness.md). The exporter refuses legacy tasks without attestations, incomplete tasks, gaps, and out-of-week due or completion evidence.

## Required decision record

The weekly review produces:

- Reporting windows reviewed.
- Missing-attribution count and the exact records requiring evidence.
- Overdue and unscheduled follow-up counts.
- Strongest recorded service-demand signal, with its count and window.
- Strongest recorded source signal, with its count and window.
- One acquisition action, one owner, one due date, and its linked Hub task.
- Any public-copy, outreach, budget, provider, or production approval still required.

## Monthly service review

Review 30-day and 90-day demand alongside currency-separated pipeline and reconciled revenue evidence. Decide whether to keep, refine, pilot, or pause a service message. A service does not become publicly proven because inquiries exist; public proof still requires verified delivery evidence and display permission.

The review must keep these evidence classes separate:

- Inquiries show recorded interest.
- Qualified leads show an evidenced fit decision and next action.
- Opportunities show defined commercial work.
- Accepted proposals show client intent, not collected revenue.
- Reconciled payments show revenue in their original currency, not profit.

## Automation boundary

The Hub may calculate counts, highlight gaps, prepare drafts, and create internal tasks after an approved action. It must not automatically send prospect messages, submit Upwork proposals, publish content, change advertising spend, or alter production/provider configuration. Those remain explicit human decisions at action time.

## Acceptance criteria

This cadence is operating—not merely documented—after four consecutive weekly reviews have:

- Used the Hub as the only action tracker.
- Left no active lead without one owner and dated next action.
- Preserved missing attribution instead of guessing it.
- Produced one bounded acquisition action with a completed or consciously rescheduled Hub task.
- Recorded any external action as approved, declined, or still gated.
