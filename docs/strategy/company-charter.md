# Ashbi company charter

Status: Proposed for Bianca and Cameron approval  
Owner: Cameron  
Review cadence: Quarterly, before public positioning or service changes

## Purpose

Ashbi helps growing businesses create a distinctive customer experience and a more capable operating system. Human creative judgment leads the work. Modern software, automation, and AI extend that work where they solve a defined business problem and can be operated responsibly.

## Working position

Ashbi is a human-led brand, commerce, and business-systems studio for growing consumer brands and selected service businesses.

Ashbi connects four outcomes:

1. A recognizable brand, packaging system, and production-ready creative foundation.
2. A modern website, commerce experience, or custom application that supports a real customer journey.
3. Less repetitive operational work through integrations, automation, and governed AI assistance.
4. Ongoing creative, web, and systems support after launch.

This is a strategic direction, not permission to claim experience or results that have not been verified.

## Ideal customers

### Primary

Founders and small brand teams launching, repositioning, or growing food, beverage, beauty, wellness, retail, and other consumer products. The strongest fit needs two or more connected capabilities, such as identity and packaging, packaging and commerce, or launch creative and a storefront.

### Secondary

Selected service businesses with an established offer and a clear operational problem that can be improved through a modern website, portal, internal tool, integration, or bounded automation.

### Fit signals

- A named decision-maker and a real business objective.
- A defined product, service, customer, or workflow.
- Willingness to provide source material, access, feedback, and approvals.
- A need for senior hands-on creative or technical work rather than commodity production alone.
- Readiness to launch a bounded first phase and measure what happens.

### Disqualifiers

- Unverifiable or deceptive public claims.
- Fully autonomous financial, legal, production, or client-facing actions without human approval.
- Work with no accountable client owner, usable inputs, or approval path.
- Requests to expose confidential client information or reuse work without display permission.
- A timeline or budget incompatible with a safe, useful delivery.

## Strategic pillars

### Human craft

Bianca's hands-on Illustrator, identity, packaging, and production work is a central differentiator. AI can assist research and repetitive preparation, but it does not replace final creative judgment, production responsibility, or client approval.

### Modern delivery

Cameron's web and software practice supports WordPress, Shopify, Astro, Next.js, headless content, custom applications, integrations, and platform modernization. The technology is selected for the client's operating needs, not marketed as the outcome itself.

### Governed systems

Automation and AI services begin with workflow evidence, a bounded pilot, named owners, source citations where appropriate, auditability, and a manual fallback. They become a public proof point only after they have been operated and documented.

### Managed improvement

Ashbi should grow recurring revenue through clear ongoing responsibilities: creative support, website care, product engineering, automation monitoring, and systems planning. A retainer is not an unlimited queue; each plan requires capacity, response, and approval boundaries.

## Company goals

- Make Ashbi's strongest creative specialization immediately understandable.
- Add modern web, custom platform, and systems services without blurring current proof and emerging capability.
- Create one traceable journey from qualified inquiry to delivery, payment, support, and referral.
- Give Bianca and Cameron one reliable view of ownership, workload, commitments, and revenue state.
- Develop credible AI and automation offers from internal and client pilots rather than hype.
- Replace overlapping tools only after migration, reconciliation, backup, recovery, and user acceptance are proven.

## Non-goals

- Becoming a generic full-service agency for every industry.
- Leading public messaging with frameworks, CMS names, or AI terminology.
- Publishing generated case studies, testimonials, performance metrics, or client names without evidence and permission.
- Building every Hub feature before one complete customer journey works.
- Retiring Bonsai or Notion because replacement code exists locally.

## Success measures

Measure only after the required data is reliable and currency remains explicit:

- Qualified inquiries by source and service interest.
- Discovery attendance, proposals, wins, and disqualification reasons.
- Project value by currency and service line.
- Time from qualified lead to contract, deposit, delivery, and payment.
- Delivery estimate accuracy once complete time and cost data exists.
- Retainer conversion, retention, outstanding receivables, and client-reported issues.
- Operational defects, manual reconciliation, automation failures, and recovery time.

Run a 30-day instrumentation baseline before approving numeric growth targets.

## Approval gate

Bianca and Cameron must approve the working position, audiences, capability boundaries, and ownership charter before this document controls public copy.
