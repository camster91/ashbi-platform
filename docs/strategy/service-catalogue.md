# Ashbi service catalogue

Status: Proposed. Capability states require approval before public use.

## State definitions

- **Current:** Ashbi can responsibly scope and deliver it now. Public examples still require evidence and display permission.
- **Pilot:** Available only as a bounded, human-supervised engagement with explicit learning and support boundaries.
- **Planned:** Directional; do not sell or imply availability.
- **Retired:** No longer marketed or started for new clients.

## Service architecture

### 1. Brand and packaging

State: Current  
Primary owner: Bianca

Customer problem: The product lacks a distinctive, coherent, or production-ready visual system.

Modules:

- Brand identity and visual language.
- Packaging concepts and systems.
- Dieline application, SKU rollout, and production artwork.
- Creative direction and launch assets.
- Brand and packaging guidelines.

Qualification: product range, market, supplied claims/copy, regulatory ownership, formats, dielines, decision-makers, production partners, timing, and approval path.

Boundary: Ashbi does not invent regulated claims, approve legal compliance, or imply production outcomes it does not control.

### 2. Websites and commerce

State: Current  
Primary owner: Cameron; Bianca leads or reviews visual direction when included

Customer problem: The website does not express the brand clearly, support conversion, publish efficiently, or integrate with the way the business operates.

Modules:

- Strategy, information architecture, design, development, and launch.
- WordPress and Shopify implementation.
- Astro, Next.js, and headless CMS marketing sites.
- Ecommerce customer experience and product-content workflows.
- Accessibility, performance, analytics, maintenance, and migrations.

Qualification: audience, conversion, content ownership, commerce requirements, integrations, editing model, accessibility, analytics consent, migration, hosting, and support.

Boundary: Framework or CMS selection follows the operating requirement. Ashbi does not promise rankings, conversion lifts, or platform replacement without evidence.

### 3. Custom platforms

State: Current for selected scoped work; public evidence Partial  
Primary owner: Cameron

Customer problem: A customer or internal workflow is constrained by spreadsheets, disconnected tools, or a platform that cannot support the required experience.

Modules:

- Client and customer portals.
- Internal tools and operational dashboards.
- Product information, content, and asset workflows.
- Industry-specific web applications.
- Platform modernization, data migration, and integrations.

Qualification: users, permissions, source systems, critical workflows, data sensitivity, migration quality, availability needs, ownership, support, and exit/export requirements.

Boundary: A custom build must have a narrower first release, a named operator, monitoring, backup, rollback, and a sustainable support model.

### 4. AI and automation

State: Pilot  
Primary owner: Cameron

Customer problem: Repetitive information work, fragmented knowledge, manual routing, or reporting consumes attention and produces avoidable delay or inconsistency.

Initial modules:

- Workflow opportunity review and risk map.
- Intake, lead, inbox, or support triage assistance.
- Document and data extraction with human review.
- Internal knowledge retrieval with sources.
- Reporting, alerts, and suggested next actions.
- Human-approved integrations and agents.

Qualification: current workflow evidence, frequency, cost of error, data sources, permissions, sensitive data, reviewer, fallback, expected volume, monitoring, and success test.

Boundary: No unreviewed financial, legal, production, credential, destructive, or client-facing actions. No time-saving or revenue claims before a measured pilot.

### 5. Managed support

State: Current for design and website support; Pilot for managed automation  
Primary owner: Named per plan

Customer problem: The client needs accountable continuity after launch rather than disconnected emergency requests.

Modules:

- Ongoing design and production support.
- Website maintenance and improvement.
- Managed product engineering.
- Automation monitoring and improvement.
- Quarterly creative, product, or systems planning.

Qualification: included systems, capacity, priorities, response expectations, approval rights, access, monitoring, rollover, exclusions, and exit process.

Boundary: Every plan has finite capacity and service boundaries. Pricing and hours live in an approved rate card, not in this strategy document.

## Offer ladder

1. Fit call or qualified introduction.
2. Brand, website, commerce, or systems opportunity review.
3. Bounded sprint or scoped first project.
4. Full brand, commerce, or platform engagement.
5. Managed creative, website, product, or systems partnership.

The fit call is not free speculative work. The opportunity review becomes paid when it produces material research, architecture, recommendations, or reusable deliverables.

## Cross-sell logic

- Identity can extend into packaging, commerce, and launch creative.
- Packaging can extend into production artwork, product-content systems, and commerce.
- A website can extend into maintenance, analytics, conversion improvements, portals, or integrations.
- A custom platform can extend into managed engineering and systems planning.
- Automation begins after the underlying workflow and ownership are clear; it is not added merely because AI is available.
